A focused Roon experience

The library deserves its own screen.

RoonDeck turns a Raspberry Pi and touchscreen into a permanent, room-ready music interface. It starts quickly, stays out of the way and keeps the controls that matter within reach.

  • Now Playing, transport and queue
  • Albums, artists, genres and playlists
  • Search, discovery and recommendations
  • Rich album and artist information

Alpine-first

A complete appliance, not another desktop.

The recommended build uses a compact Alpine Linux base, a native GTK4 interface and Cage/Wayland. There is no browser kiosk to maintain and no general-purpose desktop sitting underneath it.
